[ 02 — Agents ]

When four minds
work at once.

Keep scrolling and the field surrenders to gravity — just like Quadlet's agents surrender a single trip to four specialists. Researcher, budget planner, itinerary builder and safety checker each work on their own job, in parallel, until the energy finally settles into one merged plan.

01Parallel
4 agents · 1 goal

No agent waits for another. They run at the same time and file separate reports.

02Synthesizer
Conflict rule · open cuts

Over budget? Priciest activities go first, then stay steps down, then food goes local-first.

03Fallback
One fails, three continue

A failed agent never crashes the trip. Its section is marked missing, honestly.

[ 03 — Form ]

Chaos, then form.

Out of free-fall, the field reassembles — every bubble finding its place in a single shape. That is synthesis: four separate reports becoming one trip you can actually follow. Move your cursor through it and watch the order ripple, scatter, and recover.
